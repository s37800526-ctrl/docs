Have you decided to purchase Exe 6? To purchase Lotus Administration, ensure it's from an official source. Currently, Exe 6 is exclusively sold at our **Flare Studio Hub** using **Parcel**.

When buying Exe 6, note the differences between a **group-owned** game and a **solo-owned** game.

# **Group Games**

The **owner** of the group must purchase **Lotus Administration** because we integrated a system that checks if the owner of the group owns **Lotus Administration**.

# **Solo Games**

If you're the only developer of a game you own (that means, your game is not group-owned), you can easily purchase Lotus Administration with no issues.

# **Purchase**

You can officially purchase **Lotus Administration** on our official **Product Hub** or **Parcel's Packables**.
